Blush Matcha Theme gives the browser a calm, low-contrast surface to work on. A pale matcha frame sits above a blush pink toolbar, and the new tab page opens in a soft petal white. Deep matcha labels and berry icons keep tabs, bookmarks, and the address bar readable across every layer. Each surface is a flat, single solid color, so the window stays quiet and easy to leave open all day. The whole palette lives in the theme definition, and installing it recolors the browser chrome and the new tab page.
